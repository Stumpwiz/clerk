# Clerk Administrative Assistant Quick Guide

This is a task-oriented guide for the Residents Council Administrative Assistant and other authorized Clerk users. It
explains how to keep Clerk's organizational information accurate and how to produce the documents and lists used in
day-to-day work.

## 1. Getting Oriented / Clerk Concepts

### The organizational model

Clerk organizes roster information as **Body → Office → Person → Term**:

- A **Body** is an organizational group, such as the Residents Council or a committee.
- An **Office** is a position that belongs to a Body, such as President or Secretary.
- A **Person** is an individual represented in Clerk's organizational and roster information.
- A **Term** records a Person's service in an Office, including the service dates and, where applicable, whether it is a
  first or second term.

The Term is the central record that connects a Person to an Office. Reports, mailing lists, vacancies, and the
welcome-letter signature depend on accurate Person, Office, Body, and Term information.

### Users and People are different

A **User** is someone authorized to sign in to and use Clerk. A **Person** is someone represented in Clerk's
organizational and roster information. These are independent:

- Creating a User does not create a Person.
- Creating a Person does not give that person a Clerk account.
- A User does not need to be in the Person list. For example, someone outside the Mercy Ridge organizational roster may
  receive a Clerk account for testing or another authorized purpose.

### Main work areas

After signing in, use the Dashboard or navigation menu to open **Persons**, **Terms**, **Letters**, **Rosters &
Reports**, **Mailing Lists**, **Bodies**, **Offices**, **Users**, or **My Profile**.

## 2. People

### When to use this

Use **Persons** to add someone who needs to appear in organizational records, or to correct a person's contact
information. A Person record can hold a first name, last name, email address, phone number, and apartment.

### Add a person

1. Open **Persons**.
2. Select **Add Person**.
3. Enter the information needed for Clerk's rosters and communications.
4. Select **Create**.
5. If the person will hold an office, continue to **Terms** and create the Term. Adding the Person alone does not assign
   an office.

Clerk requires a phone number, when supplied, to contain exactly 10 digits. Clerk also prevents two Person records from
having the same combination of first and last name.

### Update a person

Find the person on **Persons**, select **Edit** (the pencil icon), update the information, and select **Update**. Correct email addresses
are particularly important because mailing lists draw addresses from Person records.

### Things to know

- The page is labeled **Persons**, although this guide generally uses “People” in ordinary language.
- Do not create a User account when the task is only to add someone to a roster.
- Delete a Person only when you are certain the record is no longer needed. Remove or correct associated Terms first.

## 3. Terms and Officeholder Changes

### When to use this

Use **Terms** whenever a Person begins service in an Office, service dates change, or one officeholder replaces another.
Clerk does not maintain a separate, manually edited “current officeholder” entry; the Terms and their dates determine
who holds an office.

### Assign a person to an office

1. Create the Person on **Persons** first, if necessary.
2. Open **Terms** and select **Add Term**.
3. Select the **Person**.
4. Select the **Office**. The list shows the Body with each Office.
5. Enter the **Start Date** and **End Date**, or select **Ongoing (no end date)** when appropriate.
6. Choose the **Term Limit Status**:
    - **None (not term-limited)** for an office without term limits.
    - **First (term-limited)** or **Second (term-limited)** when the office is term-limited.
7. Select **Create**.

Use the Person, Body, and Office filters on **Terms** to find an assignment.

### Correct dates or term-limit status

Select **Edit** (the pencil icon) beside the Term. You may change its dates, ongoing status, and term-limit status. You cannot change the
Person or Office on an existing Term. If either is wrong, delete the Term and create the correct one.

### Change an officeholder

1. Find the incumbent's Term on **Terms** and select **Delete** (the trashcan icon).
2. Create the successor as a Person if necessary.
3. Select **Add Term** and create the successor's Term with the correct Office, dates, and term-limit status.

This delete-and-create procedure changes the Person-to-Office connection. Editing a Term is only for dates and
term-limit status.

## 4. Vacancies

Clerk represents a vacancy by assigning the special Person **`(Vacant)`** to the Office through a Term. The
**Vacancies** report finds these vacancy Terms.

### When an incumbent leaves

1. Open **Terms** and delete the incumbent's Term.
2. Select **Add Term**.
3. Choose **`(Vacant)`** as the Person and choose the vacated Office.
4. Enter the appropriate dates.
5. For a term-limited Office, preserve the appropriate ending date of the term and choose the appropriate **First** or
   **Second** term-limit status. Do not assume every vacancy is a first term.
6. Select **Create**.

### When the vacancy is filled

1. Delete (using the trashcan icon) the **`(Vacant)`** Term for the Office.
2. Create the successor on **Persons** if necessary.
3. Create the successor's Term with the correct Office, dates, and term-limit status.

Do not leave both the **`(Vacant)`** Term and the successor's Term in place for the same Office.

## 5. Welcome Letters

### Standard operating workflow

1. Marketing notifies the Administrative Assistant of a new resident.
2. Approximately 30 days later, open **Letters** and generate the welcome letter.
3. Take the letter to the next monthly Residents Council meeting.
4. Have the Residents Council President sign it.
5. Deliver the signed letter to the resident's mail slot.

### Generate a letter

1. Open **Letters**. The page heading is **Letters and Template Management**.
2. Under **Generate Letter**, enter the **Letter Date**, **Recipient (s)**, **Salutation**, and **Apartment**.
3. Enter **Street Address (Optional)** and **City, State, ZIP (Optional)** only when the standard address should not be
   used. When left blank, Clerk uses 2525 Pot Spring Road, Timonium MD 21093.
4. Select **Generate Letter**.
5. Under **Available Letters**, select the generated PDF and select **View** to review or print it.

Enter the salutation without “Dear”; Clerk adds that word to the letter.

### What Clerk does automatically

Clerk automatically finds the current Residents Council President and adds that President's name and apartment to the
signature block. Keep the President's normal Person record and Residents Council President Term accurate so the
signature stays current automatically.

Do not put the President's identity in the editable letter body.

### Change the standard wording

Each incoming Residents Council President may specify changes to the standard wording.

1. On **Letters**, find **Welcome Letter Body**.
2. Select **Edit Letter Body**. The dialog is titled **Edit Welcome Letter Body**.
3. Edit the standard message in plain text and save it.

The changed body will be used for later letters. Review wording changes carefully because this is a shared standard
template.

## 6. Rosters and Reports

Open **Rosters & Reports** to refresh reports from current Clerk data and view the resulting PDFs. The current UI
provides:

- **Short Form Roster** — condensed names and offices, no personally identifiable information.
- **Long Form Roster** — fuller member details, i.e., email, phone number, apartment.
- **Vacancies** — Offices represented by a **`(Vacant)`** Term.
- **Expirations** — Terms expiring in the current calendar year.
- **Terms Report** — Terms with valid expiration dates.

### Refresh and view a report

1. Make the needed Person and Term updates first.
2. Under **Refresh Reports**, select **Refresh** beside the report you need. Refreshing overwrites the prior generated
   version with current data.
3. Under **Available Reports**, select the PDF.
4. Select **View**. Use the PDF viewer's controls to print or save a copy if needed.

### Publish the website roster

Refreshing **Short Form Roster** also automatically updates the roster published on the MRRA website. There is no
separate publication step.

## 7. Email Lists

Open **Mailing Lists** to produce address lists intended mainly for copying and pasting into Outlook or another email
message. Blank addresses are omitted and duplicate addresses are removed.

The current UI provides four lists:

- **Committee Chairs**
- **Committee Secretaries**
- **Hall Reps**
- **Residents Council Officers**

### Generate and use a list

1. Correct the relevant Person email addresses and Terms first.
2. Under **Refresh Mailing Lists**, select **Refresh** beside the desired list.
3. Under **Available Mailing Lists**, select the list and select **View**.
4. Copy the addresses from the plain-text list and paste them into the email message.

### Current selection rules

- **Committee Chairs** selects current officeholders in an Office titled exactly **Chair** in a Body other than
  Residents Council.
- **Committee Secretaries** selects current officeholders in an Office titled exactly **Secretary** in a Body other than
  Residents Council. The Residents Council Secretary belongs on the Residents Council Officers list instead.
- **Hall Reps** selects people with an active Term in a Body whose name contains “hall rep,” regardless of
  capitalization.
- **Residents Council Officers** selects all current officeholders in the **Residents Council** Body. This includes
  elected and appointed officers, the Administrative Assistant, wing representatives, and the Executive Director through
  the Residents Council **Staff** assignment.

## 8. Bodies and Offices

Bodies and Offices define Clerk's organizational structure. Creating or changing them is infrequent structural
maintenance, not routine roster work. Most Administrative Assistant tasks should use the existing structure.

### Add or change a Body

On **Bodies**, use **Add Body** to create a group. A Body has a name, an optional mission, and a precedence value that
controls ordering in reports and pages. Use **Edit** to correct an existing Body.

### Add or change an Office

On **Offices**:

1. Select the Body in the left panel.
2. Select **Add Office**.
3. Enter the Office title and precedence value.
4. Save the Office.

Use **Edit** for later changes. Office precedence controls the Office's display order within the Body.

### Things to know

- Use the exact established titles—especially **Chair**, **Secretary**, and **President**—because reports, mailing
  lists, and welcome letters use those titles.
- Before deleting a Body or Office, check for Terms that use it. Structural deletion can disrupt rosters, reports,
  mailing lists, and welcome letters.
- Create a new Body or Office only for a genuine organizational change, not merely to assign a new officeholder.

## 9. Users and Passwords

All authenticated active Clerk Users have the same access, including access to **User Management**. Clerk does not
provide different privilege levels.

### Add a User

1. Open **Users**. The page heading is **User Management**.
2. Select **Add User**.
3. Enter the User's **Email**, **Display Name**, **Password**, and **Confirm Password**.
4. Leave **Active** selected if the person should be able to sign in immediately.
5. Select **Create User**.

Email addresses must be unique. Passwords must contain at least eight characters. Creating the User does not create a
Person record.

### Edit or deactivate a User

Select **the pencil icon** beside the account. **The pencil icon** allows changes to **Display Name** and **Active** status; it
displays but does not allow changes to the email address.

There is no **Delete User** operation in the GUI. When someone should no longer have access, edit the User, clear
**Active**, and save. An inactive User cannot sign in or access protected Clerk functions.

### Reset another User's password

1. On **User Management**, select **the key icon** beside the User.
2. Enter and confirm a specific new password of at least eight characters.
3. Select **Reset Password**.
4. Communicate the new password directly to the affected User.

Clerk does not generate or email a temporary password.

### Change your own password

1. Open **My Profile**.
2. Under **Change Password**, enter **Current Password**, **New Password**, and **Confirm New Password**.
3. Select **Change Password**.

The new password must contain at least eight characters. Use **My Profile** for your own password; use **User
Management** only when assigning another User's password.

## 10. Useful Conventions / Things to Know

- Update the underlying Person and Term information before refreshing a report or mailing list.
- A current officeholder is determined from the Term connecting the Person and Office; there is no separate officeholder
  record to edit.
- Use **Ongoing (no end date)** only when an Office assignment genuinely has no known ending date.  These are all offices except those that are term limited: Residents Council officers and Wing Reps.
- Use **First** and **Second** only for term-limited Offices; use **None** for other Offices.
- Preserve exact Body and Office names used by Clerk's automated features: **Residents Council**, **President**,
  **Chair**, and **Secretary**.
- The special **`(Vacant)`** Person represents an unfilled Office. Do not create a different spelling or another vacancy
  Person.
- Generated reports replace the prior generated version. Review source records before selecting **Refresh**.
- User accounts and Person records remain separate even when they represent the same individual.
